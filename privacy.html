Memizer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Memizer (“the App”) is a personal content-publishing tool operated by the owner of the “Genitori Millennial” social media accounts. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

Who we are

The App is operated by an individual creator to publish the creator’s own short-form video content to the creator’s own connected social media accounts. Contact: giacomodonelli@gmail.com

What the App accesses

When you connect a social account (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube), you grant the App permission to read your basic public profile information (such as account name and ID) and to publish video content to your own account on your behalf. The App only acts on content that you explicitly choose to publish. It does not browse, read, or collect content from other users.

What we store

The App stores the access credentials (authorization tokens) needed to publish on your behalf. These tokens are kept privately on the operator’s own device and are used solely to publish content that you initiate. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data retention and revocation

Tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the App. You can revoke the App’s access at any time from the security/connected-apps settings of the respective platform (e.g., TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Once revoked, the App can no longer access your account.

Third-party platforms

Publishing happens through the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
